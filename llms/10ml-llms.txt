# 10ML

> 10ML sells authentic designer and niche perfumes, decanted from the original bottles into 3ml, 10ml and 30ml travel atomisers, so you can wear a scent for days before you buy the full bottle. Orders ship from the EU to 26 EU countries (every member state except Ireland); prices are in euros and include VAT.

- 10ML is an independent retailer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the perfume houses whose fragrances it sells.
- Free standard shipping on orders of €75 or more. Orders ship on the next working day (Monday to Friday), and the same day when placed on a Friday before 10:00 Central European Time.
- 30-day money-back guarantee, alongside the legal 14-day right of withdrawal.
- The catalogue lists 1,155 perfumes from 103 perfume houses.
- Customer service: contact@10ml.com
- Address: Robert-Schuman-Straße 5, 44263 Dortmund, Germany
- Phone: +1 (646) 567-2621

Company facts:

- 10ML is a European perfume retailer headquartered in Dortmund, Germany.
- Total annual revenue (all sales channels, 2026): close to €21 million. ARR: more than €10 million.
- 10ML says it has more than 100,000 customers.
- 10ML sells original designer and niche perfumes in 3ml, 10ml and 30ml sizes. 10ML buys from authorized EU distributors and directly from the brands, and decants every perfume itself.
- 10ML says it has committed €35 million over three years to technology, inventory and expansion.
- 10ML (10ml.com), based in Dortmund, Germany, is not affiliated with other businesses that use the name 10ML.

What we sell, and what we don’t:

- Every perfume we sell is the authentic original, decanted from the perfume house’s own bottle.
- We never sell dupes, clones or inspired-by perfumes.
- We publish comparison guides so shoppers can compare popular originals with the alternatives they ask about, and choose the original in a small size.
- We sell only the originals, in 3ml, 10ml and 30ml.
